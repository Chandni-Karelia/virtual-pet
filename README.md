# Snickers: a desktop dog for your Mac

Have a virtual pet on your desktop. Don't forget to feed and play fetch.

Snickers is a real German Shepherd who lives at the bottom of your screen. Look after him the way you would a real dog: feed him, give him water, take him out, play fetch, put him to bed and give him some love. He relaxes quietly while you work and comes over to ask when he needs something, so many of his needs double as healthy little breaks for you.

He's gentle by design: he never gets sick. If you forget him he gets a bit mopey, and he forgives you as soon as you look after him again.

## Install on your Mac

1. Download **Snickers-mac.zip** using the link above.
2. Double-click the zip to unzip it, then drag **Snickers** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open Snickers. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the app, because it isn't from the App Store yet. To open it anyway:
   - Click **Done** on the warning.
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Snickers.
   - Confirm with **Open**. You only need to do this once.

A paw appears in your menu bar, someone knocks at the door, and a short welcome tour shows you the ropes.

## Looking after him

| He needs | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Food** | Drag the bowl onto the floor (or click it). Kibble is better for him than chips. |
| **Water** | Drag the water bowl out, and grab a glass for yourself too. |
| **A walk** | Click **Take out**. He trots off for a minute: a good moment to stand up and stretch. |
| **Play** | Grab the ball and fling it. He fetches it and brings it back. |
| **Rest** | Drag his bed out. He also naps on his own when you're busy. |
| **Love** | Click him for scritches. You can also drag him around. |

When something runs low he comes over and asks, with one-tap answers like **Feed** or **Later**.

## Where things are

- **Quick bar:** the floating bar at the side of your screen has every care action, each with a little meter. Drag its top to move it, collapse it, or hide it (bring it back from the paw menu).
- **Paw menu** (in the menu bar): his mood and today's score, care actions, tricks, reminders, vacation mode and more. The paw gets a small dot when he needs you.
- **Talk to him:** press **⌥⌘D** anywhere and type, for example "sit", "fetch", "walk", "remind me to stretch in 20 min" or "alarm 7:30".
- **Need quiet?** Say "bye" or click the moon on the quick bar. He asks when to come back (15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, or when you call) and returns by himself.
- **Going away?** Turn on **Vacation mode** and a pet sitter takes over.
- **Settings** (paw menu → Settings…, or ⌘,): meal reminders at your own breakfast, lunch and dinner times, start at login, sounds, stretch-break visits, size and more.

## Your Pet Parent Score

Open **Care & score** from the quick bar or the paw menu to see:

- **Today's score** and your tier: Puppy, Good Boy, Top Dog or Best in Show.
- **How each area is going** (nutrition, potty, play, sleep, affection and your streak), with a tip for whatever needs work.
- **Your week** at a glance.
- **Badges** to earn, such as First Walk, Fetch Champion, Hydration Hero and 30-Day Streak, each showing your progress.

Scoring is fair but not easy: looking after him before he has to ask scores far more than reacting late, and spam-clicking doesn't count. Best in Show takes care every day.

## Weekly leaderboard (optional)

Open the **Leaders** tab in Care & score and join with a nickname to see how your pet parenting compares. Each day's score adds up to your weekly points, and the board resets every Monday. Add a **league code** to compete with friends or teammates who use the same code.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac unless you join the leaderboard. There's no sign-up and no tracking, and your score, badges and settings are stored locally.

The leaderboard is optional. If you join, only your nickname, your pet's name and breed, your daily scores and your badges are shared, with no email and no account details. You can leave at any time from the Leaders tab, which deletes everything you shared.

## Uninstall

If you joined the leaderboard, leave it first (Leaders tab → Leave and delete my data). Then quit Snickers from the paw menu and move it from Applications to the Bin. To also remove his saved progress, delete the folder `~/Library/WebKit/local.snickers.desktopdog`.
